# Judge findings on round 1 (two blind judges, baseline vs I-014 at 020b679)

- Judge 1: targets 8/8 (B1 clear, rest slight), 0 clear losses: adopt.
- Judge 2 (file times reset, told to weigh clutter and pixelation): targets 3/8 (A3, B1, G4 slight wins), G3 slight loss: do not adopt.
- Result reviewer: "holds with caveats"; A1's change is an edge-on smear; possible novelty bias.
- Decision: not adopted. Fix round requested.

## Defects named by the judges and the reviewer (all from images only)
1. **Confetti at mid and far range (G1, G3, burst-u62):** about 8–12 characters scattered across one district at random heights, sizes and colours, with 城 repeated up to four times and 山 three times. It reads as labels, not a composed sign. This cost G3 (loss) and G1 (tie).
2. **Wrong reading order:** in the aerial burst the large characters read 城…不…夜 left to right, which is nonsense to a Chinese reader. G2 and G4 also show partial or out-of-order phrases.
3. **Pixelation:** the large 夜 in A6 has stair-stepped strokes like a low-resolution bitmap font. Pixelation is the user's top complaint.
4. **Cramped or clipped on landmark towers (G2):** 城 is squeezed against a tower edge; 里 sits above a partly hidden character on a narrow face.
5. **Edge-on smears (A1, A3, G3):** characters seen at grazing angles become bright, unreadable smears.
6. **Uneven sizes within a phrase (B1, B3):** 千 is smaller and lower than 里江山 and reads as a stray dot from far away.

## What worked
B1 千里江山 reading in order; A3 crisp, few, legible characters on the hero crowns; G4 roughly reading 不夜城 with a Chongqing neon feel.
