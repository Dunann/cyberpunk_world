### Strongest reason it will fail
At A1, one of the two poses the adoption rule needs (2/3 of the targets means both A1 and A2 whether or not A3 qualifies), the only visible change is thousands of larger pink-red dots. The spec's sizes are wrong. The eval camera has a 1.05 rad vertical FOV (`controls.js:29`, `dev.js:38`), so the focal length is about 1,241 px. A1 is about 244 m from the front line, so a 1 m lantern is about 5 px (the spec says 9), a 3 m banner about 15 px (spec 27), and a 0.5 m plaque character about 2.5 px. People are not drawn there at all, because crowd meshes stop at 210 m at q2 (`crowd.js:606`) and A1's nearest facade is more than 210 m away. So at A1 the plaques become plain gilt bands, the banners become 3–4 px slivers, the extras are invisible, and what is left is the lantern strings: lanterns 0.75–1.1 m tall at 1.3–1.8 m spacing, often in two rows, which is a near-continuous emissive band about 5–8 px tall under every lit eave. They use the existing lantern shader (`signs.js:213-252`, emission `body*3.2` on (1, 0.16, 0.05)) and pass through the hue-preserving neutral tonemap with highlight desaturation (`post.js:77-90`). That is the same path that turns today's 0.44 m EMIT lanterns into the "pink dots" in A2 (crop checked: pink, not red). Nothing in the spec changes the colour response, so the most likely result at A1 is the same pink dots, bigger and three to four times as dense. The spec itself names this "Christmas lights" risk. The quick test would catch it at A2, but A1 does not appear in its go rule.

### Other concerns
- **Serious: the shared streams break as specified.** The dressing pass is "called from where the complex's parts and slots are appended", which is `finishComplex()`. But `generate()` runs `genInfill()` after `finishComplex()` (`buildings.js:77-80`, with a comment saying this order exists so seeds are kept). Parts without an explicit seed get `(index * 7.31) % 997` (`buildings_gen.js:32`). That covers most `genUrban` parts, which the infill uses (`buildings_infill.js:32`). So two of the plan's steps shift the index of every infill part and change their seeds, in the I-016 parks and infill that won G2, G3 and B3: removing the roughly 700 `lantern` props from the complex's late list, and appending roughly 1,500 props before the infill. The fix: append after `genInfill`, and replace each removed lantern with a zero-size placeholder (as `placeComplex` does, `buildings_landmarks.js:569`), or keep the props and hide them. The step 5 identity check would catch this, but the plan builds the fault in. The spec's "Shared random streams" section claims the opposite.
- **Serious: there are no gallery posts.** String tie points ("every 3.2 m"), wall lamps ("every second gallery post") and banner bracket poles ("from a gallery post") all assume posts. In I-015 a gallery is the roof of the unit below plus a box railing (`buildings_stilts.js:402`, `813-815`). The only columns are in the level-0 toe frame (`:996`, `:1085`). The cord is 0.03 m and drawn only within 120 m, which is about 0.4 px at A2's 95–110 m. So in every eval view the strings would be sagging rows of lanterns with no visible support. That is the "detached elements" defect the user ranks second. Either hang the strings from hooks on the eave underside (and say so), or add posts, which is a structural change the spec rules out.
- **Moderate: the "pent or roof-edge eave" choice conflicts with the 2.1 m rule.** The mid-level pent eave's lower edge is at y0+2.75 (`eave(..., y0+3.2, 0.9, 0.45)`, `:822`). A scale-1.5 lantern measures about 1.58 m from hook point to tassel, plus 0.6 m of sag, so its bottom is about 0.6 m above the gallery floor. Only the roof-edge eave works (lower edge at y0+6.25), and the second row then bottoms out at about y0+3.17. The spec should say "roof-edge eave only" and what roofed top units get instead.
- **Moderate: clipping that the clearance check cannot see.** A string 0.15 m in front of the roof-edge eave, with lanterns of radius up to 0.5 m, intersects that unit's own eave plate and gold strip (y0+5.95 to y0+6.27, d = −1.3) at the zero-sag tie points. `blocked()` skips pieces of the same group (`q.grp !== grp`, `:340`), so the spec's "Safety" step will not catch it. Hang points need about 0.6 m of horizontal clearance, or about 0.35 m lower.
- **Moderate: the quick test uses eval poses.** It captures A2 and A1 and makes A2 part of the go rule. I-015 was tuned only on held-out cameras, and its implementer notes that the instructions forbid capturing eval poses (I-015 `DEVIATIONS.md`). Use a held-out camera set at A2-like and A1-like distances instead. Step 0's `?hydmask` capture of all 14 poses should be done by the coordinator, not the implementer.
- **Moderate: the complex's sign lights change.** Each existing blade adds a light candidate (`signPlace.js:286`), and the complex gets 64 of them (`signs.js:437`). Turning 75% of shop signs into plaques, banners and lantern columns removes most of those candidates, unless the new forms add their own. The coloured spill on the galleries that is visible in A2 today (green, blue and magenta pools) would shrink, and the stack would read as a more uniform gold, against the judges' preference for neon. The step 5 claim that "lights [are] identical" is also inaccurate for lights inside the complex. Say whether plaques and banners emit candidates, and keep the light count.
- **Minor: the lantern pipeline needs work the spec does not count.** `lanLayout` binds only a vertex-stage storage buffer (`signs.js:392`), so glyph lanterns need the atlas and a sampler in the lantern fragment stage, and that pipeline is shared with the city and park lanterns. Lantern styles are 0 red, 1 orange-gold and 2 pink (`signs.js:243-245`), so white paper needs a new style. City lanterns cull at 300 m (`signs.js:468`), and park lanterns are a separate `Cells` (`:429`), not a cell group. The text "group(i) = 3" does not match the code; use a separate `Cells` with its own distance.
- **Minor: the mesh size is understated.** The lantern mesh is about 80 triangles of body plus four prisms, about 140–150 in total, not about 90. Even so, 3,500 instances is about 0.5M triangles, well within budget. The GPU section is otherwise credible, and the frame has about 1.4 ms of headroom.
- **Minor: "unit records" do not exist yet.** Occupancy, variant, crest and family are locals inside `unit()` (`buildings_stilts.js:741-780`), and the clearance hash is local to `hongyadong()`. The pass must add a records export to I-015's file, so it is not true that the pass "only reads". That is fine if no draws are added, but say so.
- **Minor: plaza walkers.** Mode 0 always takes `terrainHeight` (`crowd.js:47-48`). Plaza walkers must be mode 1 with an explicit y, or they stand on the terrain under the plaza.
- **Minor: the targeted complaint is misdescribed.** The plaque "replaces the current sign over the door", but the complex has no over-door slots. All 88 slots are vertical blades hanging 0.95 m out (`:871-875`). The plaques are new slots.
- **Note: the critic goal.** A2's critical defect (the blank embankment slab) and the window module limit any A1 or A2 critic score this idea can reach. The pairwise rule does not depend on them, but the 9+ target does.

### What would make it stronger
- Fix colour before size. Calibrate the lantern emission and the paper shading (a dim red shell with a bright warm core and a lit band) so the tonemapped result is red with a gold core, not pink. Check it on a held-out A1-distance camera before anything else. Make string density depend on distance from the eave, for example one row and 2.2–2.6 m spacing on normal units, and two rows only on bright shop runs.
- Hang the strings from visible hooks or brackets on the eave underside, with the cord thickened for distance (a minimum of about 1 px, as the program does for the glints), so nothing floats.
- Append every new part after `genInfill()`, and keep removed lanterns as placeholders. State this in "Shared random streams" and prove it with the existing index-by-index identity check.
- Replace A1 and A2 in the quick test with held-out cameras at 90–110 m and 230–260 m, and make the go rule need a win at both distances.
- Keep the complex's sign-light count: give plaques and banners warm light candidates in the own pool.
- Re-derive every pixel size with the real focal length (about 1,241 px at 1440p), and drop the A1 claims for people and legible plaques.
- Either raise the crowd mesh distance for the gallery agents only (to about 300 m, using LOD2), or accept that the extras help only A2 and say so.
