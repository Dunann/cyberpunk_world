### Strongest reason it will fail
The terrain here is a cliff, not a slope, and the spec's parameters were chosen for a slope. I probed the site (`stilts` site at x 240, z −140, inland normal about (−0.95, 0.30)). Along that section the ground rises from about 6 m at the bank point to about 27 m at 20 m inland, 71 m at 40 m and 96 m at 60 m. That is an average gradient of about 1.5 (56°) and up to 2.2 (66°) in the upper half. The same holds from z −340 to +40. With H = 7.2 m and the top snapped to the crest, that gives about 12 levels, not 5–9. The upper bands are 3–4 m deep, so the "merge if shallower than 4 m" rule fires there and produces 14.4 m rises. Each upper row would then be a 7.2 m strip of two-floor houses below a 7.2 m masonry riser. At night, roughly half the upper face becomes dark masonry with a strip of lit windows in front of it. That is close to the critics' complaint ("stuck onto a black wall", "floating strips of windows"), only now with texture. Also, every band platform is filled down to the ground, so the solver rarely has a gap over 3 m. Stilt frames would appear almost only under the level-0 gallery. The Hongyadong look (diaojiaolou timber frames stacked on the rock) would be largely lost. The spec has no rule for steep sections, such as taller units of 3–5 floors against the rock face, or stilt frames instead of fill on the lower levels. So the blind judge may see "tidier, but still a dark wall with rows of lights".

### Other concerns
- **Raffles tower 4 is on the cliff inside the site.** `raffles()` puts tower 4 at about (215, −68), hw 15, with ground at about 60–80 m. Its footprint spans x 200–230, which is the upper cliff face, inside the complex's z range (−280..0). Today it plunges straight into the rock, the "towers end at the hill crest with no podium" defect in A1 and A2. The spec only says to "stop at footprints". That cuts a 30 m notch through the upper galleries in the middle of A1 and A2 and leaves the tower base unresolved. The generator needs a general rule for landmark footprints that cross bands: a masonry podium, with galleries wrapping or ending on a stair.
- **The Raffles radius test would delete the plaza.** Step 7 tests the plaza against "the `raffles` landmark radius". That radius is 190 m around (220, −60), which covers the entire complex. Test the actual tower footprints instead, or there is no top plaza and no base for the pavilion towers.
- **Art direction on the eval cameras is tuning to them.** Cost and Risks both plan "art-direction passes on A1, A2" and call art direction on those captures "essential". Nothing is keyed to coordinates, but choosing parameters, seeds or pavilion and lift placement by how they look in A1 and A2 is still fitting to the test set. Require all art direction on at least three non-eval cameras (different distances, sides and heights). Look at A1 and A2 only once, in the final check. Place towers and the lift by rule (for example the ends and the crest high point) with a fixed seed. Do not pick seeds.
- **Parts and level counts are underestimated.** 12 levels × 300–420 m of galleries means 3.6–5 km of gallery. Brackets, posts and lanterns every 2.5 m alone come to about 4–6k props, before units, roofs, stilts, masonry, retaining walls, laundry and plants. Expect roughly 8–12k parts, not 4–6k. This does not threaten the gate: the real total is about 134k parts (the `buildings.js` header says ~50k, which is stale), so even 12k is about +9% of `bld-cull` (about +0.015 ms).
- **GPU: the claim holds for the gate, but the quick test does not measure it.** The fly-through path (`dev.js` PATH) passes about 170 m inland of the complex at 60–80 m height, heading north-west. It sees the complex edge-on only briefly on the approach from z +650. The fly-through average cost should be under 0.1 ms, well inside the 1.36 ms headroom, and p99 and worst frame are not at risk. At A2 (camera about 95 m away) roofs within about 65–95 m switch to the K48×32 mesh (about 3.2k triangles, drawn in both the depth prepass and the shading pass). About 60–100 such roofs give 0.4–0.6M triangles, in line with the spec. But the quick test's "+0.3 ms gbuffer at A2" runs on the skeleton without dressing, galleries or lanterns, which are most of the parts. Repeat the check on the full build. Also time the whole fly-through after adding `MAT.MASONRY` and the WOOD variants to the uber-shader. Extra branches in the facade shader can raise register pressure and slow every building pixel, not only the complex.
- **Lights.** Confirmed: lights wanted are 1934 against a budget of 1450, so priority-3 lights do displace shop spill lights elsewhere, and "net zero" is right. It does put about 16 more lights into the lighting tiles at A1 and A2 than today (about 50 new against about 34 removed). That is small.
- **Contradictions and gaps in the plan.**
  - The 2.2 m cantilevered gallery breaks the solver's own rule (cantilever > 1.5 m is "not allowed").
  - Stairs are promised in the Idea and in Risks, and are one of the critics' named missing items, but no implementation step builds them.
  - Unit depth is never specified. That matters when bands are 3–7 m deep.
  - The level-0 gallery "doubles as the riverside walk", but `terrainBank.js` already builds a promenade and lamps at the same place. Say which one wins.
- **A3.** The bare mound in A3 is mostly the face north of the complex. The 420 m length cap and 40 m of retaining walls past each end probably will not reach it. Treat A3 as a likely tie, not "a clear gain". With A1 and A2 winning, adoption still works (2/3).

### What would make it stronger
- Add a steep-section rule driven by the measured gradient. Where the band depth is under about 5 m, replace terrace rows with a continuous multi-storey timber frame stacked against the rock (3–5 floors per unit, galleries every two floors). On the lower 2–3 levels, use real stilt frames over the slope instead of masonry fill. Keep masonry for plinths and the crest. Give the masonry share of the face as a target, for example under 25% of the projected area from a bank-level camera.
- Correct the level count (about 12 here) and the parts estimate. Re-check the quick-test GPU limit on the dressed build, and add a fly-through bench to the quick test.
- Handle landmark footprints that cross bands with a general rule (a podium or plinth, with galleries ending on a stair). Test the plaza against footprints, not the Raffles radius.
- Move all art direction to non-eval cameras. Place landmark pieces by rule with a fixed seed. Add a step for stairs (one flight per about 40 m and level) and a unit-depth rule. Make the gallery cantilever consistent with the solver (bracket props count as support, up to 2.5 m).
