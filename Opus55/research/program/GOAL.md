# Goal: Neon Confluence improvement program

## Problem
Neon Confluence (`Opus55/`) is a real-time WebGPU cyberpunk river city that runs in the browser. It performs well, but strict reviewers rate most views as good indie quality rather than showcase quality. Improve its visual quality, coherence and excitement, and keep the GPU performance.

## The user's priorities, in order
1. **Buildings.** They are too boxy and pixelated and need more character. Make a few places look great rather than the whole world look just okay.
2. **Detached elements.** Some things don't look properly attached, especially the Hongyadong-style stilt complex on the Jade River cliff.
3. **Land merging.** There are odd forested strips between the city and the water, which real cities never have. Turn them into parks with character, or bring the city to the water, or a mix of both. It should read as one city with many cultural influences, not separate cities.
4. **Signage diversity.** Add some very large, striking signs, and skyscrapers with text light shows.
5. **More technology.** Monorails, other aircraft and spacecraft.
6. **Detail everywhere.** It should look sleek and be exciting to explore. Render distance can change if necessary.

## Focus zones, in priority order
- **Zone A, the Chongqing core:** the Yuzhong peninsula tip and the confluence, the Hongyadong cliff, the hillside streets and the monorail.
- **Zone B, the Bund and CBD waterfront.**
- **Global views:** the overlook, the city-to-water transitions, an aerial view of the city.
- **Later, if budget remains:** the steppe quarter, the gardens.

## What "reached the goal" means
Every zone reaches a mean of 8 or more on the strict rubric (`eval/critic-rubric-v1.md`), averaged over two critics and all four score axes, with no defect above minor.

## Constraints
- **GPU budget (hard gate, RTX 4050 Laptop, 2560x1440, scripted 60-second fly-through):** average ≤ 11 ms, 99th percentile ≤ 16.7 ms, worst frame ≤ 25 ms. The baseline averages 9.64 ms. Every idea's spec must say where any extra GPU time comes from.
- **Functional checks:** all 18 must pass, with no WebGPU errors.
- **Single file:** the result stays one self-contained `index.html`, everything generated by code (no image, model or sound files), WebGPU with no engine.
- **Landmarks:** inspired by real towers, never replicas.
- **Git:** commit and push often, and never commit `out/`, `node_modules/` or `eval/runs/`.

## Budget
- Stop when the 7-day usage reaches 25%, enforced by the usage-budget hook. The weekly window resets on 2026-11-24 at 13:00 UTC; after the reset, the 25% stop is re-applied to the new week.
- At most 5 agents run at once. Evaluations run one at a time on a quiet GPU.
