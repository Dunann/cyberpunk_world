# What both baseline critics found (eval v1, baseline at 84bfb94)

Zone means: A 5.33, B 5.34, G 5.12 (8 is the goal). The two independent critics agreed on these points.

## Biggest problems (in both reviews)
1. **Bare hills and cliffs.** The terrain under the Hongyadong stilt complex (A1, A2) and the peninsula tip (A3) is a smooth, featureless dark slab or mound. The stilt houses look stuck onto a black wall, with no stilts, stairs, retaining walls, terraces or walkways. Upper rows read as floating strips of windows. Towers end at the hill crest with no podium or plaza.
2. **Repeated stilt modules.** The same eave, window and lantern module is copied 80+ times in a regular grid. Heights, widths, roof forms, balconies, signs, laundry, AC units, timber and 3D lanterns need to vary.
3. **Not one continuous city.** Districts are islands on flat plinths with cake-like straight edges, separated by wide bands of repeated blob-tree forest and an empty central plain (G2, G3, B3). The city looks flat, not "stacked on hills". Bridge ends land in forest without roads (B3). A huge empty flat plaza sits beyond the burst-u12 intersection. Quays are plain bands with no steps, moorings, boats or piers.
4. **Boxy, repeated buildings.**
   - Towers are box extrusions with the same tan window texture and the same small cyan/white pyramid crowns everywhere (A6, G1).
   - Window textures look like pixel noise or are at the wrong scale (B2, A6).
   - The cylinder tower in B4 has stripe seams, and one tower shows moiré.
   - Podiums are stacks of boxes. Crowns, setbacks, materials and rooftop equipment need variety.
5. **Visible placeholders up close.**
   - A giant dark purple box fills the B2 foreground: a vehicle or barrier at the camera.
   - Flat shopfront glass panels (A4).
   - Blocky cars, mannequin-like people (B1).
   - A chunky plain railing and flat floor (G1).
   - The cable car is a box with cut-out passengers (G4).
   - Plain grey monorail beams with no piers or joints, and black voids under the hillside (A5).
6. **Foliage and reflections.**
   - Low-poly blob trees.
   - Wet-street reflections swim, with dark patches moving between frames (burst-u12).
   - Laser reflections on the river are a dithered checkerboard (burst-u62).
   - An orange fog volume changes shape between frames.
   - Near cars break up at the frame edge.
7. **Missing items from the brief.**
   - Mountains are barely readable behind the city.
   - Central Asian / Mongolian elements are only a tiny far-away dome district.
   - Few holograms or drones at street level.
   - The lattice TV tower reads as a near copy of a real tower.

## What works (keep it)
- **Rainy neon hillside street with Chinese signs (A4):** the best mood.
- **Liziba monorail through a building (A5):** iconic.
- **Bund drone dragon and laser show (B1).**
- **Cableway, pink bridges and searchlights (G4).**
- **Warm stilt complex and its reflection:** works as a concept.

## The user's own priorities
These overlap with the findings above:
1. Buildings with character, not boxy or pixelated. Polish a few places first.
2. Attach things properly, especially Hongyadong.
3. Merge the land: no forest strips between the city and the water. Use parks with character, or bring the city to the water.
4. Diverse, very large signage, and text light shows on towers.
5. More technology: monorails, aircraft, spacecraft.
6. Detail, sleekness, excitement.
