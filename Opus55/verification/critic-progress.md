**Verdict after round 4: FAIL.** Four viewpoints score at least 8 on every axis. Every critical defect from earlier rounds is gone, but some major defects remain.

## Open items after round 4, ranked by the critic
1. **The city reads as flat.** It should visibly climb hills in Chongqing fashion. The mountains also need more light and haze layering. This is a world-layout change and affects most viewpoints.
2. **Building geometry and silhouettes.** Facade depth on hero towers, stray coloured stripes on the round tower (viewpoint 7), and more distinctive skyline silhouettes.
3. **Lake gardens (viewpoint 5).** Layout (the axial lamp-lined path should give way to a winding route and a large lake in view), foliage crispness, and the moon gate opening.
4. **Mountain edge (viewpoint 9).** The foreground rail band, flat colourless lighting, the tree carpet and the hard fog edge.
5. **Empty areas.** The plaza beyond the burst intersection, the right side of viewpoint 4, and oversized shopfronts in viewpoint 3.
6. **Near-camera people.** Closer detail, and more pose variety.
7. **Wet surfaces and visible rain everywhere.**
8. **Structural detail.** Bridge underside and piers, the monorail guideway, the yurt roof.

## Declined
The critic asked for exact copies of real towers in rounds 1–2. This was declined: by the user's direction, landmarks are inspired by real towers, not replicas.
